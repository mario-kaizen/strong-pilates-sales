What This Means

Kaizen launched STRONG Yorkville's paid acquisition campaign on February 23, 2026. Within two full weeks of running, the results are clear.

Intro Offer purchases more than doubled. The pre-Kaizen 12-week average was 17.2 per week. In our first two full weeks, that jumped to 36 per week — a 110% increase. This is the top-of-funnel engine working.

Account creation is up 34%. More people are signing up, creating accounts, and entering the system. The pipeline is fuller than it's been since opening.

Memberships and packages are steady — and that's expected. The 87 intro offer purchasers since takeover are still working through their 5–15 day experiences. The conversion wave into memberships and packages will follow over the next 2–4 weeks as these intro offers complete.

The first takeover week (Feb 23–Mar 1) was intentionally lower — the campaign was in its learning phase. By week two, the ads found their audience and the numbers reflect it.

The system is building momentum. More intro offers today means more memberships next month. That's how compound growth works.
