What this means

Kaizen launched STRONG Yorkville's paid acquisition campaign on February 23, 2026. Within two full weeks of running, the results are clear.

Intro Offer purchases more than doubled. The pre-Kaizen 12-week average was 17.2 per week. In our first two full weeks, that jumped to 36 per week, a 110% increase. This is the top-of-funnel engine working.

Account creation is up 34%. More people are signing up, creating accounts, and entering the system. The pipeline is fuller than it has been since opening.

Memberships and packages are steady, and that is expected. The 87 intro offer purchasers since takeover are still working through their 5 to 15 day experiences. The conversion wave into memberships and packages will follow over the next 2 to 4 weeks as these intro offers complete.

The first takeover week (Feb 23 to Mar 1) was intentionally lower. The campaign was in its learning phase. By week two, the ads found their audience and the numbers reflect it.

The system is building momentum. More intro offers today means more memberships next month. That is how compound growth works.

The current automation sequence

Here is every automated touchpoint a new intro offer purchaser receives. The sequence runs for about 15 days, then stops entirely.

Day Channel Content
0 SMS Welcome. Arrive 15 min early, bring grip socks.
0 Email "Your pass is active" + SPRINT challenge (5 classes = $50 credit)
0 SMS "First class: smashed it. Book your next class"
~1 Email "Georgia" personal check-in (automated template)
~1 Email Class types exploration
~5 Email STRONG brand story
~7 SMS "You're halfway through"
~8 Email SPRINT completion. $50 off code (SPRINT2026)
~15 Email "Your pass is complete. Stay STRONG with a membership"
16+ Nothing Sequence stops entirely. Total silence.

What is wrong with this sequence

  1. The close email is weak. "What's next? Stay STRONG with a membership or class pack. STRONG Starts Here [link]." No urgency, no personal touch, no specific recommendation.
  2. Zero follow-up after expiry. Total silence after Day 15. The contact disappears from the system entirely.
  3. Broken automations. The halfway SMS reads "You're halfway through your !" with a missing package name. The merge field is broken.
  4. "Personal" emails are automated templates. The "Georgia" check-in goes out at the same time with the same structure for every single person. It does not feel personal.
  5. Converters got the exact same sequence. The automations did not convert them. Something else did. The sequence is not the sales engine, it is background noise.

Recommendations

Seven actions to close the conversion gap. Ordered by impact.

1

Route all calls and SMS through Grow

Every conversation should be tracked. If it is not in the system, it did not happen. This is the foundation for everything else.

2

Build a post-expiry conversion sequence (Days 16, 18, 21, 25)

The current sequence stops at Day 15. That is when the real sales process should start. Four touchpoints over 10 days to re-engage and close.

3

Add a high-attendance trigger at 5+ visits

153 people attended 6+ classes and nobody closed them. Trigger a personal outreach from Hayley when someone hits 5 visits during their intro.

4

Fix broken automations (merge fields)

The halfway SMS has a broken merge field showing "You're halfway through your !" with no package name. Audit and fix all merge fields.

5

Customise copy to Hayley's voice

The current emails feel generic and corporate. Every touchpoint should sound like it is coming from a real person who owns the studio.

6

Strengthen the conversion email

Replace the generic "Stay STRONG" email with specific recommendations based on attendance, time-bound incentives, and social proof.

7

Evaluate ClassPass ROI

530 contacts, zero memberships. ClassPass fills classes but does not build a member base. The revenue per visit needs to justify the seat it takes from a potential member.

SMS Email

A close needs three things: a specific recommendation, a reason to act now, and a way to say yes easily. This touchpoint delivers all three, two days before expiry so there is still time to decide.
